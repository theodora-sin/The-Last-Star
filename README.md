## Introduction
A thrilling story-based decision game where every choice shapes your path--and where you'll need to piece together a hidden word to unklock the true ending.
Each person has its own beginning and an end, but no one knows when their final moment will come. 
Every decision, every step you take are going to shape your future. 
Inside every person, there is a shiny star shining through their life. As long as hope remains, the star burns brightly. When hope is lost, the star slowly fades into darkness, turning positive energy into dark energy. Hope is contagious--but so is despair, people surrounding don't just affect themselves, they influence the people they meet and the world around them.

## How to Play
--choose your character and begin the journey
--Make choices at each story point--every decision affects what happen next
--Collect gems along the way, each hiding a letter of a secret word
--At the end, guess the hidden word to determine your true ending. 

##Controls:
--Mouse/tap: buttons and story choices
--Arrow keys or drag the sliding object using touchscreen in maze and catch the gem
--Arrow keys or touch the inbuilt arrow key button to play the snake
